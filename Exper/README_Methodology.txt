Computed directly from Exper.zip. One CSV row per experiment, per layer, and per layer/token cell.
Bootstrap-interval exclusion is descriptive and uncorrected; no claim of significance under multiple testing.
Same-backbone layer profiles aligned on model_layer. Pearson/Spearman are descriptive, not inference about random independent layer units.
Peak effect is max absolute Cohen d from layer_effects.csv; token peak from token_layer_effects.csv.
Models have different numbers of analyzed layers; do not compare raw layer indices as homologous across sizes.
This analysis does not evaluate held-out prediction, calibration, or the separate PRDetect/RepreGuard benchmark tables.
